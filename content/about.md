Income For Everyone is a daily publication about the collision between artificial intelligence, automation, job security, and income policy.

The site tracks concrete signals: layoffs tied to AI strategy, automation-led restructuring, labor-market data, institutional research, and policy proposals that affect workers' economic security.

The point is not to predict a single dramatic employment collapse. It is to document the uneven pressure already showing up across companies, sectors, and public data, then connect that evidence to the case for stronger income floors, including Universal Basic Income.

Daily posts are short by design. Each one links to source reporting or official data and closes with the policy implication to watch.

## Editorial approach

Income For Everyone publishes this site and maintains its automated publishing workflow. AI tools search for sources, draft daily articles, and prepare video narration. Automated checks enforce article structure and source restrictions; they do not establish that every claim is accurate or that an article has received human review.

Source notes describe the evidence recorded for an article. Missing notes mean the information was not recorded, rather than that a review was completed. Analysis about AI and income policy should be distinguished from the facts reported by linked sources.

## Corrections

To report an error, [open a correction request](https://github.com/asobel12321/incomeforeveryone/issues/new) with the article URL, the disputed statement, and supporting evidence. Please omit personal or confidential information from public requests. Substantive corrections should be identified in the affected article with the correction date and what changed.
